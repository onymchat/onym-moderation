# Evidence rules

## What a report must carry

A report is refused at intake unless it is signed by a reporter holding
a live mandate with this authority, names a class this authority
publishes, names an accused who also holds a live mandate, and carries
**authorship proof** for the material it discloses.

The last is the one that matters. Anyone can paste text and attribute
it; the proof is what distinguishes evidence from an assertion about
evidence. Material without it is not weighed.

## What the authority does not do

It does not scan devices or conversations, obtain keys, or see anything
a recipient did not choose to disclose to it. Evidence reaches this
authority in exactly one way: a recipient of the material discloses it
in a report.

This is a real limit on what can be proved, and the rules above are
written knowing it. An authority that cannot see a conversation cannot
verify a denial either, which is why an ambiguous case resolves to no
violation rather than to a guess.

## Joining, rather than multiplying, cases

At most one case is open per accused per class at a time. A further
report about the same accused and class joins the existing case instead
of opening a second one, and joining serves the accused with the new
allegations and restarts their response window against them.

The decision deadline does not move when a report joins. Otherwise a
case could be walked forward indefinitely by adding to it.

A report that arrives after the decision deadline has passed does not
reopen a case the contract has already ended.

## Reporting in bad faith

A dismissed report lowers the filer's intake weight; an upheld one
raises it. See [reputation](./reputation.md). There is no bounty and no
payment for reporting, deliberately: paid reporting industrialises
false accusation.

## What the accused may file

A signed statement and counter-evidence, at any point during the
response window, as many times as the bounds in
[confidentiality](./confidentiality.md) allow. Silence is not a
confession, and answering early does not shorten the window — the time
was promised, not the opportunity.

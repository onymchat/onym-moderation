# Child sexual abuse material (`csam`)

This is the rule a decision is made against. It is not a summary of one
— it is transcribed from the Onym Reference Moderation Policy §2.1, it
is what a human reviewer applies on appeal, and where a model is used
it is handed these same words.

**Violation.** The authenticated material depicts, describes, solicits,
distributes, or facilitates sexual material involving a person under
18.

**Not a violation merely because of its subject.** A good-faith safety
report, victim-support communication, lawful evidence handling, news
report, documentary, or medical discussion.

**Required context.** There must be evidence supporting both the sexual
nature of the material and that the person is under 18. If either is
absent, ambiguous, or rests only on an unsupported assertion, the
outcome is no violation.

Read that third paragraph as carefully as the first. A rule that says
only what a violation *is* will find violations everywhere; the
required context is what makes an absent or ambiguous element resolve
to "no violation" rather than to a guess.

## Why 365 days and not permanent

The reference policy permits a permanent ban for this class. This
authority does not impose one.

A permanent sanction is valid only while an independent appellate
authority can hear an appeal against it (§6, and again in §8: "an
existing ban remains only while a living declared forum can hear an
appeal"). No such forum is declared, and this authority's own moderator
is not one — reviewing appeals against your own verdicts is the thing
an appellate exists to prevent.

Declaring a permanent ban anyway would publish a sanction that the
interface is required to clear. That is worse than the finite term in
both directions: a promise that is not kept, and in practice no ban at
all. If an independent appellate is established, a new manifest can
raise this term, for mandates taken from that point onward.

## Emergencies

Nothing here limits lawful emergency reporting or anyone's right to
contact public authorities. If material involves a child at risk,
contact the appropriate authorities in your jurisdiction directly — do
not wait on a case here. See [lawful
reporting](./lawful-reporting).

# Lawful reporting

Nothing in this authority's terms limits lawful emergency reporting, a
reporter's right to contact public authorities, or any person's legal
rights.

Filing a report here is not a substitute for contacting law
enforcement, and it is not a precondition for doing so. If material
involves a child at risk or a credible, imminent threat to someone's
safety, contact the appropriate authorities in your jurisdiction
directly. Do not wait on a case here — the response and decision
windows published for each class are measured in days, and they exist
to give an accused person a fair chance to answer, not to hold up an
emergency.

## What a mandate is not

A mandate is consent to be judged by this authority under the published
classes. It is not consent to unlawful processing, and it is not a
waiver of any right of appeal.

## What this authority may be required to preserve

Where law specifically requires this authority to preserve or report
material, it will, and it may keep that material beyond what a case
otherwise requires. That is the only circumstance in which material
outlives the purpose it was disclosed for — see
[confidentiality](./confidentiality.md) and the
[retention schedule](./retention.md).

Preservation works by a **hold**, placed when the material arrives and
released on a date fixed at that moment. A hold outranks every retention
period: nothing deletes held material, not the scheduled sweep, not the
case ending, not a later change of terms.

## What happens to preserved material

Three things, and it is worth being exact because the alternatives are
things people reasonably assume.

**Nobody here looks at it.** It is not displayed in the moderator panel
and it is not shown to any classifier. The normalized copy that would
make either possible is destroyed as soon as the class is known; only the
original is kept, sealed, for the referral.

**It is not classified, and the case ends in dismissal.** A case under a
preservation hold is not decided by a model here, and no moderator can
decide it on the material either, because the material is not displayed.
Nothing therefore decides it — and this authority's rule is that an
undecided case is dismissed at its decision deadline, with the
case-open mark cleared.

So a referred case ends with no sanction from this authority. That is
worth stating plainly rather than leaving to be inferred: the referral
is the consequence that matters, and it is independent of the case. This
authority is not the body that acts on this material, and a ban it
issued would be neither the remedy nor a substitute for one.

**It is referred, and the referral does not wait for the case.** Response
windows and appeal windows exist to give an accused a fair chance to
answer. They are not a reason to delay a referral, and a deadline that
held one up would be the case machinery working against the thing it is
for.

This authority does not submit the referral itself. It builds a signed
package — the digests, the metadata, the case record and the sealed
material — and an operator submits it through the channel they are
registered for, then records the reference that comes back. Exporting
that package is the one path by which the material leaves this service,
and each export is written into the case history.

## Which classes this applies to

Only classes for which the manifest publishes both a preservation period
and a referral procedure. **This manifest publishes neither, for any
class.**

That is a deliberate refusal rather than an omission. A class that would
require preservation — `csam` — does not accept image evidence here at
all: a report naming one is refused, and the bytes are deleted before the
refusal returns. Text reports for the class are unaffected.

The reason is readiness, not a view about the reports. Taking custody of
that material means being equipped to preserve it, refer it, account for
it, and destroy it on a schedule — and an operator who accepts it without
being a service provider with the protections and the reporting
relationship that role carries may be committing an offence simply by
holding it. This service therefore refuses by default, and will only
accept such material where an operator has published the terms under
which they can lawfully hold it.

If material involves a child, contact the appropriate authorities in your
jurisdiction directly. Do not route it through here and do not wait on a
case.
